# Independent review

- Review ID, round, date, reviewer actor / model / session
- Candidate artifact ID, version, manifest digest
- Mandate, criteria, source inputs, and scope
- Actual methods and evidence inspected
- Findings with stable IDs, impact/severity, reproduction or source, expected correction, and owner
- Verdict and acceptance-blocking conditions
- Limits, skipped verification, dissent, and decision references
- Independent retest evidence for corrections
